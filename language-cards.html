About

Single-page flashcard app. Storage: browser localStorage under ukr-cards-v3. Translation: built-in dictionary, then the preferred provider, then the other if the result looks wrong. Click any translation to edit; ↻ re-translates that cell. No accounts, no telemetry.

What this app does

A flashcard study app for learning Ukrainian vocabulary, with parallel translations into Russian, English, and Finnish. Each card has up to four languages; quizzes prompt in one language and ask you to recall another. Progress is tracked per card using a simple SRS (spaced-repetition) schedule, stored locally in your browser. Works offline once loaded.

The five tabs

📝 Words ❓ Quiz 🗂 Decks ⚙︎ Settings ⓘ Help
  • Words — add new vocabulary (auto-translated), search and filter the full pool, export/import/clear.
  • Quiz — pick a deck, view its contents, configure a session, hit Start. Where you actually study.
  • Decks — manage all decks: rename, duplicate, delete (per row), bulk delete, merge into new or existing.
  • Settings — quiz direction, audio, target languages, export/import/sync data, keyboard shortcuts.
  • Help — this page.

On phones the tab bar sits at the bottom of the screen; on desktop, at the top.

Other common scenarios

Study a built-in category (verbs, food, weather, …)

  1. On Quiz, click the deck picker.
  2. Scroll to the Categories (read-only) section and pick one — these auto-update from the pool.
  3. Click ▶ Start session.

Pick what to study with the Categories filter

Within the active deck, the Categories strip on the session card lets you scope a session to specific tags (e.g. only verb cards in a mixed deck).

Filter the word pool (Words tab)

  1. On Words, scroll to All words.
  2. Search any language, or use the Category / Status / Sort drop-downs. Status Missing translations finds cards that need Translate missing.

Review only what's due

  1. On Quiz, tick Due only (the count next to it shows how many).
  2. Start a session — only cards past their SRS interval will be quizzed.

Select multiple cards at once

Tick checkboxes in Contents or Add cards from pool to bulk-remove, bulk-add, or move/copy to another deck. Shift-click selects a range.

Change the quiz direction

On Settings: Prompt language = what you see, Answer language = what you must produce. Default: Ukrainian → English.

Back up or move your data

On Settings → Data, click Export full state for a JSON of cards, decks, and progress. Import… restores it on another device or browser.

Pull in newly bundled words

On Settings → Data, click Sync with bundle. Merges new cards from the latest shipped bundle without touching your progress.

Tips

  • Keyboard: the session is fully keyboard-driven — see Settings → Keyboard shortcuts.
  • Audio: press S or click the 🔊 button to replay the prompt.
  • Offline: after the first load the app is cached; works without a network. Translation requests still need the internet.
  • Dark mode: follows your OS preference automatically.
  • Inline rename: click the deck title in the Quiz header — or any name in the Decks list — to edit in place.
  • Long-press a card row on touch devices to remove it from a deck.
  • Edit a translation: click the Russian / English / Finnish cell in any word list. Enter saves, Esc cancels. ↻ re-translates that cell from Ukrainian (dictionary first, then Google / MyMemory).
  • Cancel a session: cards you already answered keep their SRS update; only unanswered cards are skipped.
  • Privacy: nothing leaves your device except translation queries when adding new words — Google / MyMemory, or your own translation server if one is deployed.
  • Storage: everything lives in browser localStorage under ukr-cards-v3. Reset Everything (Settings → Data) wipes it.
